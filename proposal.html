★ The Scope

The Do's.
And the Don'ts.

Fifteen honest hours need honest boundaries — written down, so month three never becomes a debate.

✅

In scope — the Do's

Every week
  • B2B prospecting, relationship upkeep & warm handoffs to Karsen
  • Growth Command Center — full pipeline visibility, from first contact
  • 3–4 posts per week, captured on-site during shift hours
  • Short-form video editing, done by me
  • Google profiles & local SEO across all three houses
  • Baseline dashboard + one-page monthly report
  • 30-minute weekly check-in, one point of contact
  • Big-moment pushes: Oktoberfest, holidays, launches
🚫

Out of scope — the Don'ts

Protects the sales hours
  • Daily community management or same-day reactive posting
  • Heavy video production — long-form, motion graphics, brand films
  • Paid ads management — unlocks after Day 90, inside a cap you approve
  • Evening & weekend coverage — swapped hour-for-hour, agreed in advance
  • Committee-routed approvals — one contact keeps us fast
★ The working rule

New requests are trades, not additions — something comes in, something moves out that week.
