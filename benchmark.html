3

Trillium lost the Greenway — and opened three downtown sites instead

Their beer garden ran 2017 to 2025 and did not return; the Conservancy picked Boston Harbor Distillery. Trillium answered with an indoor winter garden at Winthrop Center, an outdoor one on Devonshire, and a waterfront garden at the Boston Harbor Hotel. The after-office corridor is being contested right now.

0

Jack's Abby's parent bought them in October 2024. The founders left. Events now funnel through a five-venue portfolio with a shared sales team. Their own website still calls the company “proudly independent” — two years on.

3am

Social districts ran on Temple Place through July with an extended last call and the street closed to cars. Downtown spending rose 15% in a fortnight. Whether they return in 2027 has not been announced.

★ Checking our own work

Three claims of ours
that did not survive.

🗺️

We are near the Freedom Trail. We are not on it.

The Trail runs down Tremont, then turns onto School Street. It never enters Downtown Crossing. Say “a block off the Freedom Trail” and it holds. Say “on” it and the first person with a map takes the rest of the deck less seriously.

📊

There is no single Boston vacancy rate

Same quarter: Cushman & Wakefield say 19.0% with falling absorption, JLL say 23.6% with the first back-to-back positive quarters since 2019. Different inventory. Never quote one unattributed.

⚽

Summer 2026 is not a normal comparison year

The World Cup lifted Massachusetts beer sales 12% and on-premise 27% — the biggest jump of eleven host cities. But hotel occupancy was flat: it displaced normal visitors rather than adding new ones. Any 2027 forecast built on this summer will overshoot.

Finding these ourselves is the point. A benchmark that only flatters us is a brochure.

The lever that works on a winter is pulled in summer

Corporate business pre-sold in June to September is the only thing that fills January. That makes the summer prospecting block the highest-leverage stretch of the whole year — and it is the one we are standing in.

Do not let a hotel number talk us out of December

That 61% counts people sleeping in hotels. Holiday parties are booked by local companies whose staff sleep at home. Two different businesses, two different numbers.

🎨 Brand & Content

One brand across three houses, and whether anyone can tell the story apart from the beer.

★
🎨 Engine 01 · Brand & Content

The only authentic ownership
story left in Boston beer.

✅ Going for us
  • Night Shift belongs to the state's largest brewer and its founders are gone. Trillium is founders and a farm. Lamplighter holds community and values — from Cambridge. In Boston proper, worker-owned is ours alone.
  • That is not sentiment. It is the thing that sells to an HR or DEI budget holder choosing between four rooms that all serve good beer.
  • The content bar is on the floor. Lamplighter's entire blog holds three posts — two of them job listings.
⚠️ Broken
  • Three houses, one voice. Trillium and Night Shift each run a handle per venue. Our Eastie regular and our City Hall tourist get each other's content.
  • The December 2024 guidelines name two houses. There are three.
  • We cannot benchmark anyone's cadence — including our own. There is no baseline until 28 August.
→ What we do
  • Split the houses, keep one voice. One shoot feeds three feeds, cut differently — that makes three cheaper to run than one, not dearer.
  • Extend the guidelines to the whole estate before anything else gets designed.
  • One engineered announcement per season instead of posting into the void — Night Shift's May launch landed on five outlets the same day.

📍 Engine 02 · Digital & Traffic

Six thousand people arrive

✅ Going for us

→ What we do
  • Three landing pages — corporate, private parties, non-profit — on keyword URLs, one set per house. Cheapest, highest-return item in this benchmark.
  • Claim the free B2B directories this month.
  • Rebuild all three Google profiles before Oktoberfest, not after.

🤝 Sales & Partnerships

★
🤝 Engine 03 · Sales & Partnerships

The most in-demand slot in the market
is the shift we already work.

✅ Going for us
  • 53% of employees want the party between noon and 5pm. 56% prefer a Friday. We are staffed Tue–Thu 10–2 and Fri 11–2 — no evening cover, no scope renegotiation.
  • 51% of decision-makers are raising budgets; food and drink budgets are up 31% year on year.
  • The mid-size downtown room is genuinely unclaimed.
⚠️ Broken
  • Harpoon already holds Boston Common — with the City Parks Department and Emerson College's stage. Our City Hall Plaza plank and our Emerson plank both walk into the incumbent.
  • We are late for part of December. Parties book four to six months out; this season is being bought now and largely closes in early October.
  • The 150–250 buyout is the slowest door to knock on first.
→ What we do
  • Publish the rate card this week. Capacity, a number, a guest floor, a named human, 24 hours.
  • Lead with a 15-guest product, not a 250-guest one.
  • Work Fidelity and TD Bank in September, by name.
  • Sell the seasons nobody fights over: Q1 offsites and the 2027 holiday calendar.

★ Two things to say out loud

Better we raise these
than the room does.

🏛️

Harpoon has the Common, the City and Emerson

Their beer garden runs May to late October on the UnCommon Corner, free admission, with the Parks Department and a 5,000 sq ft Emerson stage that WERS broadcasts from.

Our answer is not a bigger lawn. It is the private room, three neighbourhoods, and being the only place in Boston where the money stays with the people pouring the beer.

🗓️

A share of December 2026 is already sold

Events of a hundred or more book four to six months ahead. 86% of companies want December. Working backwards, this season is being bought right now and closes in early October. We started on 10 August.

Setting an ambitious December number would be programming the Day-90 review to fail. Target what is still winnable and put the weight on Q1 and 2027.

🍺

And the Oktoberfest calendar is crowded. Sam Adams goes mid-to-late September, deliberately ahead of Harpoon, whose 35th year lands the first weekend of October. Our 19 September launch sits on top of Sam Adams — but as of today nobody has announced 2026 dates. The window to move first is open right now.

★ What it means

Six decisions for
28 August.

1

The audience splits in two

B2B prospecting Tue–Thu into staffed offices. Paid consumer calendar Thu–Sat. No Monday launches, ever.

2

Oktoberfest moves first or takes second

Announce before Sam Adams and own the front of the season, or deliberately pick a different weekend. Not the same week, hoping.

3

December gets an honest target

State plainly what is already gone. Weight the plan toward Q1 offsites and the 2027 calendar.

4

The entry product is 15–30 people

A fixed-price afternoon package, closable in one email. The buyout is the second-year outcome of that relationship.

5

Publishing the number is the strategy

Rate card, capacities, a named human, 24 hours — on a page built from the search query.

6

Rewrite the City Hall story

The City's own Plaza criteria were social impact, diversity and local ingredients. A worker-owned co-op scores well on that rubric. Press it.
