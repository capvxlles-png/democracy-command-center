Seasonal push across the houses. The party calendar sold hard while companies are still deciding. First corporate events executed and invoiced.

Week of November 9

Day 90 — a reading, not an argument

We sit down with the targets we agreed in September and the numbers this dashboard has been showing all along. Nothing in that meeting will be new information to anyone.

One thing worth saying out loud

A corporate event cycle runs sixty to ninety days from first hello to signed. Conversations starting in August become bookings in October and November. Month one looks quiet on purpose — that's the part that pays later, and it's why the Day-30 targets will measure conversations rather than contracts.
